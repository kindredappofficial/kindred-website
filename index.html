Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Kindred respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the information Kindred may collect and use when you create an account and use the Kindred dating application.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use Kindred, information may include:

  • Account information such as your email address.
  • Profile information, photos, bio, and dating preferences.
  • Location information used to help show relevant people nearby.
  • Matches, messages, and other interactions within Kindred.
  • Reports, blocks, and information submitted through safety features.
  • Notification information necessary to deliver app notifications.
  • Basic technical and service information necessary to operate the app.

How information is used

Information is used to operate Kindred, provide matching and messaging functionality, personalize the dating experience, maintain safety, prevent abuse, provide support, and improve the service.

Location

Kindred may use location information to determine approximate distance and provide location-based matching. Kindred does not intend to publicly display a user's precise physical location to other users.

Safety

Information associated with reports or blocks may be used to investigate violations of Kindred's community and safety rules and to protect users.

Account deletion

Users can delete their Kindred account through the application's account settings. Information associated with an account will be handled in accordance with applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Kindred evolves. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to kindredappofficial@gmail.com .

Delete Your Kindred Account

Kindred users can permanently request deletion of their account and associated account data.

Delete directly in the Kindred app

Open Kindred → Settings → Privacy & Safety → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps.

Can't access the app?

Send an account-deletion request from the email address associated with your Kindred account to kindredappofficial@gmail.com .

What is deleted

Account and profile information associated with the user's Kindred account is removed as part of the account-deletion process, subject to information that may need to be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, legal, or regulatory purposes.

18+ only

Child Safety Standards

Kindred is an adult dating service intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older.

Minors are not permitted to create or use Kindred accounts. Kindred maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including content or behavior that facilitates, promotes, solicits, depicts, or enables the sexual exploitation or abuse of children.

Reporting child safety concerns

Kindred users can report suspected underage users and child safety or sexual exploitation concerns directly within the Kindred app.

Review and enforcement

Child safety reports are reviewed through Kindred's moderation system and are treated as high-priority safety concerns. Accounts that violate Kindred's child safety standards may be suspended or permanently banned.

Escalation and legal compliance

Kindred reviews credible child safety concerns and complies with applicable child safety laws. When legally required or appropriate, suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation may be reported to appropriate regional or national authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children's CyberTipline for applicable United States reports.
